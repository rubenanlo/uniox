# Uniox

Uniox is a local-first desktop email and calendar client. Your mail, calendar
events, and credentials stay on your own computer — there is no Uniox server,
no account to create, and no data sent to us.

## What it does

- Connects to your existing Gmail / IMAP mailboxes and Google Calendar.
- Stores messages and events in a local database on your machine.
- Uses Google Sign-In (OAuth) only to access **your own** mailbox and calendar,
  with your explicit permission, for the sole purpose of showing them to you in
  the app.

## Privacy

Uniox does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. See the full
[Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY.md).
