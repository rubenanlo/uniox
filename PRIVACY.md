# Uniox Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-21_

Uniox ("the app") is a local-first desktop email and calendar client. This
policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Summary

Uniox runs entirely on your own device. There is no Uniox server. The app does
not collect, store, transmit, or sell your personal data to us or to any third
party.

## What the app accesses

When you choose to connect a Google account, the app requests permission to
access, on your behalf:

- **Gmail** (`https://mail.google.com/`) — to read, send, and organize the
  email in your own mailbox.
- **Google Calendar** (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar`) — to read
  and manage the events in your own calendars.

These permissions are used solely to display and manage your own mail and
calendar inside the app, on your device, at your request.

## Where your data goes

- Email messages and calendar events are stored in a local database on your
  computer.
- Your account credentials and OAuth tokens are stored in your operating
  system's secure keychain on your computer.
- The app communicates directly between your device and Google's servers. Your
  data is never routed through, or stored on, any server operated by Uniox or
  its developer.

## Data sharing

Uniox does not share, sell, or transfer your data to anyone. The app has no
analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

Uniox's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Revoking access

You can disconnect an account inside the app at any time, and you can revoke the
app's access to your Google account at
[myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
